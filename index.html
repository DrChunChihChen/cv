About Me

I'm Elvis Chen

Assistant Professor in Department of International Business at National Taichung University of Science and Technology

👋 Hello! I am a researcher and Assistant Professor specializing in Machine Learning, Circular Economy, and Sustainability. I earned my PhD from National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University in 2020 and served as a visiting scholar at North Carolina State University.

🔬 I am passionate about extracting insights from data to solve complex problems. My recent work on simulating the EU's circular economy using deep learning was funded by the National Science and Technology Council.
