ResearchTwo strands, one method: quantitative decision models

🌱 Environmental & resource economics

Circular economy, ecological footprint, energy transition, and the decoupling of economic growth from environmental pressure, using panel econometrics and system dynamics. Settings include the EU, the G20, Taiwan, and high-income and nuclear-dependent economies.

🤖 Smart manufacturing & AI agents

Production scheduling and MRP, cloud-based advanced planning and scheduling, machine learning with genetic algorithms for process optimization, and LLM-based multi-agent systems for business workflows.

Carbon accounting tool “Ni Shuo De Suan” Faculty-built

Lightweight, real-time, reduction pathways

An agile carbon-inventory and product-footprint calculator designed for small and medium-sized firms.

  • Converts activity data into emissions automatically
  • Visualises emission hotspots
  • Simulates reduction scenarios (ISO 14064)

HelloIR · institutional research platform Faculty-built

Big-data mining, ML early warning, decision dashboards

Combines higher-education data, machine-learning early-warning models and dashboards to support data-driven planning at universities.

  • Analyses student dropout risk across many dimensions

On-premise AI passport reader Faculty-built

Tested on 100 passports: 98% field accuracy

Reads passport data pages with a vision-language model running on the local machine, so images and personal data never leave the computer.

  • Runs locally: no data leaves the device
  • ICAO machine-readable-zone check digits plus a second OCR engine
  • Human sign-off, then batch export to Excel

AI career navigator for business students Faculty-built

What are AI skills worth in salary?

5,286 trade and business job ads (104 Job Bank) Open the site (Chinese) ↗

An econometric study of Taiwanese job ads for trade and business graduates, measuring what AI skills do for pay and careers.

  • 2,607 AI-related vs 2,679 non-AI postings
  • Effect of AI skills on starting pay, negotiable-high-pay offers and senior pay ceilings
  • Regional and industry premiums, plus a skills self-check and salary calculator

TradeDNA | AI listing assistant for Alibaba sellers Student project

AI knows the market. TradeDNA teaches it your company.

A Chrome side-panel extension that helps cross-border sellers prepare Alibaba listings. Unknown specs are left blank instead of invented, comparisons use transparent formulas, and a person approves every AI suggestion.

  • Imports spreadsheets and product photos and maps the fields
  • Writes bilingual titles, selling points and SEO keywords
  • Generates white-background, in-use and creative product images from the original photo
More features
  • Collects competitor listings while you browse and compares up to 8 side by side
  • Dashboard with market average price, price bands, missing-spec alerts and priority fixes

SiteCompass | Storefront history and closure-risk check Student project

Before you sign the lease, see how many shops this address has had.

A site-selection tool for people opening a shop. It links 2.7 million company registration records (2013–2026) into 2.88 million address histories and cross-checks tax, population and rent data.

  • One-sentence search: what shop, which area, what budget
  • 3D "signboard strata": one column per address, one layer per business, coloured by open, closed or moved
  • Checks whether a registered business is really operating, using invoice records
More features
  • Three-year closure-risk model, back-tested on 113,000 new shops: 6% closure in the safest group vs 36% in the riskiest
  • AI pre-lease report in which every number must cite its government source
